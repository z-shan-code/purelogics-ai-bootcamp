# 🎨 Week 9 — Generative AI: Stable Diffusion & Image Generation Workflows

> Week 9 moved from text-based generative AI into visual generative AI — understanding how diffusion models generate images from noise, and how to build practical workflows around image generation.

---

## 🧠 What This Week Covered

### 🌀 Stable Diffusion
- Studied how diffusion models work at a conceptual level — generating images by iteratively removing noise from a random starting point, guided by a text prompt
- Understood the core idea that sets diffusion models apart from earlier generative approaches (like GANs): a gradual, denoising-based generation process rather than a single-shot generator/discriminator setup
- Practiced generating images using Stable Diffusion, experimenting with prompts to control output

### 🖼️ Image Generation Workflows
- Built practical workflows around image generation — going beyond a single prompt-to-image call into a more structured pipeline
- Explored how prompt design, parameters, and workflow structure affect the quality and consistency of generated images
- Gained hands-on experience with the tooling used to work with diffusion-based generative models in practice

---

## 🧠 Key Takeaways
- Conceptual understanding of how diffusion models generate images, distinct from text-based LLM architectures covered in earlier weeks
- Practical experience prompting and working with Stable Diffusion
- Understanding of how to structure a repeatable image generation workflow rather than one-off generations
- Broadened understanding of generative AI beyond text — into the visual/multimodal side of the field
